**Tommy John, the baseball legend whose name became synonymous with a career-saving elbow surgery, has passed away at the age of 83.**

## Context / Background
Tommy John was a renowned American baseball player who played for several teams, including the New York Yankees and the Los Angeles Dodgers. However, his legacy extends far beyond his playing career. In 1974, John underwent a revolutionary surgical procedure to repair a torn ulnar collateral ligament in his elbow, which was performed by Dr. Frank Jobe. This pioneering surgery, now known as Tommy John surgery, has since become a common procedure to repair damaged elbows in baseball players.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
The impact of Tommy John surgery on baseball has been significant. According to various reports, over 500 players have undergone the procedure since its inception. The surgery has been credited with extending the careers of numerous players and has become a vital component of modern baseball.

## Quote
> "Tommy John was a true pioneer in the world of baseball, and his legacy will continue to inspire future generations of players," said a baseball official.

## What's Next?
As the baseball community mourns the loss of Tommy John, fans and players alike will continue to celebrate his contributions to the sport. The legacy of Tommy John will live on, and his name will remain synonymous with the revolutionary surgery that has transformed the game of baseball.

**The English Football Association (FA) is set to withdraw its support for FIFA President Gianni Infantino, following the scrapping of the World Cup sell-off plan.**

## Context / Background
The decision to withdraw support comes after a tumultuous period in the football governing body, with several nations expressing dissatisfaction with Infantino's leadership. The scrapped World Cup sell-off plan has been a major point of contention, with many critics arguing that it would have led to a loss of control and revenue for national associations.

**The Philadelphia Eagles have secured a major deal with defensive tackle Jalen Carter, signing him to a four-year, $152 million extension.** 
## Context / Background
The Eagles' move to extend Jalen Carter's contract underscores their commitment to strengthening their defense. As one of the most promising young defensive tackles in the league, Carter's performance has been instrumental in the team's success. This extension not only reflects his value to the team but also positions him as the highest-paid defensive tackle in NFL history.

## What's Next?
With this significant deal in place, the Eagles are poised to make a strong push in the upcoming season. The extension of Jalen Carter will not only impact the team's defensive strategy but also influence the dynamics of the NFL, particularly in terms of defensive tackle contracts. As the season progresses, fans and analysts alike will be watching to see how this investment pays off for the Eagles.

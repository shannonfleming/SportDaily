**Executive Summary:** 
Fever coach Stephanie White has received backing from players Sophie Cunningham and Caitlin Clark after a pregame rant. The coach's comments sparked a reaction from fans, with some booing her during the game.

## Context / Background
The controversy began when White made comments before a recent game, which were met with criticism from some fans. The situation escalated when fans booed her during the game, prompting a response from Cunningham and Clark.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Player | Response to White's Rant |
| --- | --- |
| Sophie Cunningham | Defended White, stating that the coach's comments were justified |
| Caitlin Clark | Felt 'sick' hearing fans boo White, and expressed support for the coach |

> 'I felt sick hearing the fans boo Coach White. She's an amazing coach and an even better person.' - Caitlin Clark

## What's Next?
The team will look to move forward from the controversy, with Cunningham and Clark's support for White expected to boost team morale. The coach's comments and the subsequent reaction from fans have sparked a wider debate about the treatment of coaches in the sport.

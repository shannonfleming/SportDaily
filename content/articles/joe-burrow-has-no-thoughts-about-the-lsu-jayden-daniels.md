**Joe Burrow, the Cincinnati Bengals quarterback, has distanced himself from the controversy surrounding Jayden Daniels and his number at LSU.**
## Context / Background
The controversy began when Jayden Daniels, the current quarterback for LSU, requested to wear the number 5 jersey, but DJ Pickett, a freshman, had already been assigned the number. This led to a heated debate between the two camps, with some calling for LSU to return the Heisman Trophy won by Joe Burrow in 2019.

> According to sources close to the matter, Joe Burrow has no thoughts on the issue and is focused on his upcoming season with the Cincinnati Bengals.

## What's Next?
The situation is still unfolding, and it remains to be seen how LSU will resolve the issue. In the meantime, Joe Burrow will be preparing for the upcoming NFL season, where he will look to lead the Cincinnati Bengals to another successful campaign.

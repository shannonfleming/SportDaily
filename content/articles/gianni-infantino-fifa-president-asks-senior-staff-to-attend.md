**Gianni Infantino, the president of FIFA, has requested the attendance of senior staff at a crisis meeting in Morocco, scheduled to take place on Wednesday.** 
## Context / Background
The meeting comes at a time when Infantino is facing increased scrutiny and pressure. Recent reports have suggested that the FIFA investment plan, which was proposed by Arsene Wenger, had to be scrapped. This, combined with other challenges, has led to a sense of urgency within the organization.

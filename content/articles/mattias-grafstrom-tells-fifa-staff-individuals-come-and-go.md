**Executive Summary**: Mattias Grafstrom, in the wake of turmoil surrounding Gianni Infantino's FIFA World Cup sell-off plan, has addressed FIFA staff by stating 'individuals come and go', seemingly downplaying the impact of key figures breaking ranks with Infantino.

## Context / Background
The statement by Mattias Grafstrom comes at a time when FIFA is facing internal conflict, with several key figures publicly disagreeing with Gianni Infantino's plans. This has led to a significant amount of turmoil within the organization, with some countries even withdrawing their support for Infantino's re-election.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Figure | Stance on Infantino's Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Arsène Wenger | Supports the plan as 'absolutely necessary' |
| Mattias Grafstrom | Downplays the impact of opposition |
| Various FIFA Staff | Mixed reactions, with some openly opposing the plan |

> The situation within FIFA is complex, with various stakeholders having different opinions on the direction the organization should take. As Grafstrom noted, 'individuals come and go', but the impact of their decisions can be significant.

## What's Next?
As the situation continues to unfold, it will be interesting to see how FIFA navigates these challenging times. With several countries and key figures already expressing their discontent, the road ahead for Gianni Infantino and his plans seems uncertain.

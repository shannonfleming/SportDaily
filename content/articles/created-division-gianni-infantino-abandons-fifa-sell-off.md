**FIFA has abandoned its plan to sell off stakes in the World Cup following a global backlash.** 
## Context / Background
The plan, which was proposed by FIFA President Gianni Infantino, aimed to sell off stakes in the World Cup to private investors. However, the proposal was met with widespread opposition from various stakeholders, including football associations and fans.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Entity | Stance on Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| FIFA | Abandoned plan |
| Football Associations | Opposed |
| Fans | Opposed |

> The decision to abandon the plan is a significant victory for those who opposed the proposal, and it highlights the importance of listening to stakeholders in the decision-making process.

## What's Next?
The abandonment of the plan means that FIFA will have to explore alternative revenue streams to support its operations. The organization may consider other options, such as increasing sponsorship deals or introducing new revenue-generating initiatives.

**Arsenal's recent performance has been marred by defensive issues, leading to their second consecutive defeat, this time at the hands of Borussia Dortmund in the Emirates Cup.**

## Context / Background
The Emirates Cup is a pre-season friendly tournament that takes place at the Emirates Stadium in London. This year's edition saw Arsenal face off against Borussia Dortmund, a German football club that has been making waves in recent years. The match was highly anticipated, with both teams looking to test their mettle before the start of the new season.

The stats show that while Arsenal had more possession, Borussia Dortmund was more clinical in their finishing, with 10 shots on target compared to Arsenal's 8.

> The defeat is a setback for Mikel Arteta's side, but it's still early days in the pre-season, and the team has time to work on their defensive issues.

## What's Next?
Arsenal will look to bounce back from this defeat in their next pre-season friendly match. The team has a busy schedule ahead, with several high-profile matches lined up before the start of the new season.

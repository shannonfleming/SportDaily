**FIFA Remains Defiant Over World Cup Investment Plan Despite UEFA Boycott Vote.** 
## Context / Background
FIFA is pushing ahead with its World Cup investment plan, despite a vote by UEFA to boycott the plan. The decision has sparked a significant backlash, with various football associations and organizations weighing in on the issue.

| Organization | Stance on FIFA Investment Plan |
| --- | --- |

> 'Nobody is selling football', FIFA insists, as it pushes ahead with its consultation plan despite the backlash from UEFA and other football associations.

## What's Next?
FIFA will continue with its consultation plan, despite the opposition from UEFA and Concacaf. The outcome of this plan remains to be seen, but it is likely to have significant implications for the future of the World Cup and the football community as a whole.

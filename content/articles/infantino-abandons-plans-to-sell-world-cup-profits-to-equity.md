**FIFA President Gianni Infantino has abandoned plans to sell World Cup profits to private equity following massive pushback from various stakeholders.**

## Context / Background
The plan, which was valued at $20 billion, aimed to sell a significant portion of the World Cup's commercial rights to private equity firms. However, the proposal was met with widespread criticism and opposition from football associations, leagues, and other stakeholders.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Entity | Stance on Plan |
| --- | --- |
| UEFA | Opposed |
| Concacaf | Opposed |
| AFC | Reacted to plan's abandonment |
| FIFA | Abandoned plan |

> 'We will continue to work together to find solutions that benefit the entire football community.' - Statement from UEFA.

## What's Next?
The abandonment of the plan is expected to have significant implications for the future of the World Cup and FIFA's financial dealings. As the football community moves forward, it remains to be seen how the governing body will navigate its financial affairs.

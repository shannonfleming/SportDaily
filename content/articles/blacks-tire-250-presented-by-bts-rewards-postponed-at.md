**The Black’s Tire 250 presented by BTS Rewards at Richmond Raceway has been postponed due to weather conditions.**

## Context / Background
The NASCAR Truck Series event, which was initially scheduled to take place on Friday, has been rescheduled for Saturday. This decision was made after weather conditions made it impossible to hold the event as planned.

| --- | --- |

> The postponement of the event has been confirmed by NASCAR officials, who are working to ensure that the event takes place as soon as possible.

## What's Next?
The event is now scheduled to take place on Saturday, and fans are eagerly awaiting the start of the race. NASCAR drivers and teams are also preparing for the event, which is a crucial part of the Truck Series season.

**Baseball has lost a legend with the passing of Tommy John, the former MLB pitcher and namesake of the pioneering Tommy John surgery, at the age of 83.**
## Context / Background
Tommy John was a four-time MLB All-Star and had a successful career spanning over 26 years. He played for several teams, including the New York Yankees and the Los Angeles Dodgers. The Tommy John surgery, which is a procedure to repair a torn ulnar collateral ligament (UCL) in the elbow, was first performed on John in 1974 and has since become a common operation for baseball players.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
The Tommy John surgery has been instrumental in extending the careers of many baseball players. Here are some key points about the surgery:
* Over 1,000 MLB players have undergone the Tommy John surgery since its inception
* The surgery has a success rate of over 80%
* The average recovery time for the surgery is 12-18 months

> 'Tommy John's legacy extends far beyond his playing career, as the surgery that bears his name has changed the face of baseball forever.' 

## What's Next?
As the baseball community mourns the loss of Tommy John, his legacy will continue to live on through the countless players whose careers have been extended thanks to the pioneering surgery. The MLB will likely pay tribute to John in the coming days, with teams and players around the league honoring his memory.

**Vita Vea, a two-time Pro Bowl defensive tackle, has requested a trade from the Tampa Bay Buccaneers due to a contract dispute.**

## Context / Background
Vita Vea has been an integral part of the Buccaneers' defense since being drafted in 2018. However, contract negotiations between Vea and the team have stalled, leading to the trade request. This move comes on the eve of the team's training camp, adding uncertainty to the team's preparations for the upcoming season.

The Buccaneers will likely explore trade options for Vea, with several teams reportedly interested in acquiring the defensive tackle.

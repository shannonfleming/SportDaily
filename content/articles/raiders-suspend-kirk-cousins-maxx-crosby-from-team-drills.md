**The Las Vegas Raiders have taken disciplinary action against two of their key players, Kirk Cousins and Maxx Crosby, following a physical altercation during a team practice session.**

## Context / Background
The incident occurred during a recent Raiders practice, with reports indicating that the scuffle between Cousins and Crosby was intense enough to require intervention from team staff and security. This is not the first time that tensions have risen during Raiders practices, but the involvement of two high-profile players has brought the situation under increased scrutiny.

> The team's decision to suspend both players is aimed at maintaining a positive and respectful team environment, and to prevent such incidents from escalating in the future.

## What's Next?
The suspensions are expected to be a short-term measure, with both players likely to return to team drills soon. The Raiders will be monitoring the situation closely to ensure that the incident does not affect team morale or performance.

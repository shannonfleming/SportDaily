**The Cleveland Browns have secured a major piece of their defense with the signing of Denzel Ward to a 2-year, $62.2M extension.**
## Context / Background
Denzel Ward has been a crucial part of the Browns' secondary since being drafted in 2018. His ability to shut down opposing team's top receivers has been invaluable to the team's success.

| Contract Value | $62.2M |
| Average Annual Salary | $31.1M |
> The deal makes Ward the highest-paid defensive back in NFL history, surpassing other top defensive backs in the league.
## What's Next?
The signing of Denzel Ward is a significant move for the Browns as they look to strengthen their defense for the upcoming season. With this extension, the team can now focus on other areas of need.

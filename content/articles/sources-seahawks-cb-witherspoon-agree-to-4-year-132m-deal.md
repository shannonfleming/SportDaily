**The Seattle Seahawks have secured their top cornerback, Devon Witherspoon, with a 4-year, $132M deal.**
## Context / Background
The Seahawks' move to sign Witherspoon to a long-term deal is seen as a significant move to solidify their defense. Witherspoon has been a key player for the team, and this deal makes him the highest-paid cornerback in the NFL.

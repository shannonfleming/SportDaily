**Manchester City have rejected Barcelona's opening bid of £38.5m for midfielder Rodri, with talks between the two clubs set to continue.**

## Context / Background
The Spanish midfielder has been a key player for Manchester City since joining the club in 2019. Rodri has made over 150 appearances for the Citizens, scoring 14 goals and providing 14 assists. His impressive performances have attracted the attention of several top European clubs, including Barcelona.

| 2022-2023 | 40 | 5 | 5 |

> 'We are confident that we can reach an agreement with Manchester City for the transfer of Rodri. We believe he is a key player for our project and we are willing to pay a fair price for his services.' - A Barcelona spokesperson.

## What's Next? (Schedule)
The two clubs are expected to continue negotiations in the coming days, with Manchester City reportedly seeking a higher fee for the midfielder. Rodri's future at the Etihad Stadium remains uncertain, with the player yet to comment on the speculation surrounding his transfer.

**FIFA President Gianni Infantino has sparked controversy by threatening countries that do not agree to the proposed World Cup sale plan, warning them that they will miss out on extra funding.** 
## Context / Background
The proposed plan involves selling the rights to the World Cup, which has been met with opposition from several countries and UEFA. The plan is part of a larger effort by FIFA to increase revenue and expand its reach. 
## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Entity | Stance on World Cup Sale Plan |
| --- | --- |
| FIFA | In favor |
| UEFA | Opposed |
| Gianni Infantino | In favor |
## Key Quote
> 'Countries that do not agree to the plan will not receive extra funding' - Gianni Infantino
## What's Next?
The situation is ongoing, with FIFA and UEFA set to discuss the plan further. The outcome of these discussions will have significant implications for the future of the World Cup.

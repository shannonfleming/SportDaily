**UEFA has announced that it has lost confidence in FIFA's president, Gianni Infantino, following the failed private equity plan for the FIFA World Cup.**

## Context / Background
The private equity plan, which aimed to invest $20 billion in the FIFA World Cup, was met with significant backlash from various stakeholders, including UEFA. The plan was eventually scrapped due to the intense criticism it received.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Entity | Stance on Private Equity Plan |
| --- | --- |
| UEFA | Opposed |
| FIFA | Initially Supported, Later Scrapped |
| Various Stakeholders | Criticized |

> UEFA's statement on behalf of its 55 national associations highlighted the lack of confidence in Infantino's leadership following the failed plan.

## What's Next?
The scrapped private equity plan has significant implications for the future of the FIFA World Cup. UEFA's loss of confidence in Infantino may lead to further changes in the leadership of FIFA.

**FIFA has scrapped its plans to invest in the World Cup after facing widespread opposition from its members and other governing bodies.**

## Context / Background
The proposal, which was put forward by FIFA President Gianni Infantino, aimed to bring in private investment to help finance the World Cup. However, the plan was met with resistance from many of FIFA's members, including the Asian Football Confederation (AFC) and the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA).

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Organization | Stance on Investment Plan |
| --- | --- |
| FIFA | Scrapped |
| AFC | Opposed |
| UEFA | Opposed |

> The decision to scrap the investment plan is a significant setback for Infantino, who had been pushing for the proposal to be approved.

## What's Next?
The scrapping of the investment plan means that FIFA will have to explore other options for financing the World Cup. This could include increasing revenue from broadcasting rights, sponsorships, and ticket sales.

**Paris Saint-Germain has secured a thrilling 2-1 victory over Aston Villa in the UEFA Super Cup, with Desire Doue scoring the winning goal.**

## Context / Background
The UEFA Super Cup is an annual association football competition contested by the winners of the UEFA Champions League and the UEFA Europa League. This year's match saw Paris Saint-Germain, the Champions League winners, face off against Aston Villa, the Europa League winners.

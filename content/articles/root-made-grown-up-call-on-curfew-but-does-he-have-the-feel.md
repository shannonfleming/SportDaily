**Joe Root has made a significant decision as the new England Test captain by abolishing the team curfew, a move that has been hailed by former captain Nasser Hussain. However, Hussain also expressed concerns that Root sometimes lacks the feel for the game as a leader.**

## Context / Background
The decision to remove the curfew is seen as a 'grown up' call by Root, indicating a shift in the team's approach to discipline and player responsibility. This move comes ahead of the three-Test series against Pakistan, a crucial series for England.

> Nasser Hussain praised Root's decision, saying it was the right call, but also expressed concerns about Root's ability to lead the team effectively, comparing him to his predecessor Ben Stokes.

## What's Next?
The England team will face Pakistan in a three-Test series, with the first match scheduled to start soon. The series will be a crucial test for Root's captaincy and the team's overall performance.

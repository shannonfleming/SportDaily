**US President Donald Trump has publicly expressed his support for FIFA President Gianni Infantino, stating that replacing him would be a 'terrible mistake'.**

## Context and Background
The FIFA president has been under pressure lately, with several organizations, including Uefa, Concacaf, and AFC, pushing for his resignation and discussing potential rival competitions. These calls come amidst accusations of FIFA breaking trust 'through deception'.

## Tactical Key Points and Current Situation
The situation is complex, with various parties involved and different interests at play. Trump's backing of Infantino adds a new layer to the scenario, potentially influencing the outcome of the current power struggle within FIFA.

> The support from Donald Trump is significant, given his position and influence, but it remains to be seen how this will affect the future of Gianni Infantino as FIFA president.

## What's Next?
As the situation continues to unfold, FIFA members are in talks over potential breakaway competitions, which could significantly alter the landscape of international football. The coming weeks and months will be crucial in determining the fate of Gianni Infantino and the direction of FIFA.

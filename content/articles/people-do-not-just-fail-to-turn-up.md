**Jermain Defoe's abrupt departure from Woking as the head coach has raised eyebrows, with the club's director of football, Jody Brown, citing 'growing concerns' about Defoe's commitment.**

## Context / Background
Jermain Defoe, the former England and Tottenham striker, took on the role of head coach at Woking just four months ago. However, his tenure was cut short, leaving the club and fans stunned. The reasons behind his exit are now coming to light, with claims of Defoe not attending a meeting and training session being a significant factor.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
Given the short duration of Defoe's stint at Woking, there is limited data on his tactical approaches or the team's performance under his leadership. However, the incident highlights the importance of commitment and communication in football management.

> People do not just fail to turn up, which is what has happened on this occasion, and we cannot continue with someone who does not want to be here.

## What's Next?
The club will now be looking to appoint a new head coach to lead the team forward. The search for a suitable candidate is expected to begin immediately, with the aim of minimizing disruption to the team's schedule.

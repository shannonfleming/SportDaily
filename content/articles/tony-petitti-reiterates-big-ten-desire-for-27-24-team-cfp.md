**The Big Ten conference is pushing for a significant expansion of the College Football Playoff (CFP) to a 24-team format by 2027.**

## Context and Background
The Big Ten conference has been a major player in college football, and its commissioner, Tony Petitti, has been vocal about the need for a larger playoff format. The current four-team format has been in place since 2014, but Petitti believes that a 24-team format would provide more opportunities for teams to compete and increase fan engagement.

## Tactical Key Points and Stats
The proposed 24-team format would likely involve the five power conference champions, as well as 19 at-large bids. This would provide more opportunities for teams from smaller conferences to compete, and would also increase the number of games played in the postseason.

> The Big Ten is committed to making the College Football Playoff a more inclusive and competitive tournament.

## What's Next?
The proposal for a 24-team CFP is still in the discussion phase, and it remains to be seen whether it will be adopted. However, with the support of the Big Ten conference and other major players in college football, it is likely that we will see significant changes to the playoff format in the near future.

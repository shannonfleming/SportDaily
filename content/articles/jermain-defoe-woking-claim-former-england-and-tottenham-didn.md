**Jermain Defoe's departure from Woking has sent shockwaves through the football community, with the club claiming the former England and Tottenham striker failed to attend a meeting and training session before his exit.**

## Context / Background
Jermain Defoe joined Woking in May, but his tenure was short-lived. The 39-year-old striker had a storied career, playing for top-tier clubs like Tottenham and Sunderland, and earning 57 caps for England. His move to Woking was seen as a surprise, but an opportunity for the club to bring in a experienced player.

Defoe's exit from Woking has raised questions about the club's management and the striker's commitment to the team. The club's director has expressed concerns about Defoe's behavior, stating that he didn't turn up for a meeting and training session before his departure.

> 'It's impossible to continue in this situation,' said a Woking director. 'We can't have a player who doesn't show up for meetings and training sessions.'

## What's Next?
Defoe's future in football is uncertain, with some speculating that he may retire or pursue a coaching career. Woking, on the other hand, will need to regroup and focus on the upcoming season, which starts in two weeks.

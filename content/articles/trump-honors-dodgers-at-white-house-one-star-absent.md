**The Los Angeles Dodgers recently visited the White House to celebrate their back-to-back World Series titles.**
## Context / Background
The Dodgers' visit to the White House is a tradition that dates back to the early 20th century. The team has visited the White House on several occasions, including after their World Series wins in 1955, 1959, 1963, 1965, 1981, 1988, 2020, and 2021.

The Dodgers' recent visit to the White House was to celebrate their back-to-back World Series titles in 2020 and 2021.

> The Dodgers are one of the most successful teams in Major League Baseball history, with a record 20 National League pennants and 7 World Series titles.

## What's Next?
The Dodgers are set to begin their 2023 season in March, with their first game scheduled against the Arizona Diamondbacks. The team is expected to be a strong contender for the National League West division title.

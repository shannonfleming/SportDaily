**The University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill has placed its football general manager, Michael Lombardi, on paid administrative leave.**

## Context / Background
Michael Lombardi, a former Browns executive and longtime Bill Belichick confidant, has been placed on paid administrative leave by the University of North Carolina. This decision has been reported by multiple news outlets, including WRAL, ESPN, and NBC Sports.

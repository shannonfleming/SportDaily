**The first day of practice at the 2026 Hungarian Grand Prix has concluded, with Ferrari's Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton leading the pack.**

## Context / Background
The Hungarian Grand Prix is one of the most challenging tracks on the F1 calendar, with its tight corners and limited overtaking opportunities. Friday's practice sessions gave teams a chance to fine-tune their cars and get a feel for the track.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Driver | Team | Best Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charles Leclerc | Ferrari | 1:16.543 |
| Lewis Hamilton | Mercedes | 1:16.654 |
| Max Verstappen | Red Bull | 1:16.721 |

> 'It's been a good day, but we know that Saturday is when it really matters. We need to stay focused and keep pushing to improve our performance.' - Charles Leclerc

## What's Next?
The teams will return to the track on Saturday for qualifying, where they will battle it out for the top spots on the grid. With the competition expected to be fierce, every thousandth of a second will count.

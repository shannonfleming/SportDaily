**FIFA is facing intense criticism from major football confederations, including UEFA, AFC, and CONCACAF, over a failed proposal to sell a stake in the World Cup.**

## Context / Background
The controversy surrounds a proposal by FIFA to sell a stake in the World Cup, which has been met with fierce opposition from the aforementioned confederations. They have accused FIFA of a 'breach of trust' and 'deception' over the handling of the proposal.

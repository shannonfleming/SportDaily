**The Czech Football Association president has expressed support for FIFA President Gianni Infantino's plan to sell World Cup stakes to private investors for $20 billion.**

## Context / Background
The plan, which has been met with criticism from various football associations, aims to generate significant revenue for FIFA. The proposed sale has sparked debate within the football community, with some expressing concerns about the potential impact on the sport's integrity.

## What's Next?
The plan's fate will be decided in the coming weeks, with FIFA and various football associations set to discuss the proposal in more detail. The outcome of these discussions will have a significant impact on the future of the World Cup.

**European football's governing body, UEFA, has announced a united front against FIFA's plans for the World Cup, threatening a boycott if the proposed changes are implemented.**

## Context / Background
The dispute centers around FIFA President Gianni Infantino's plans to increase the frequency of the World Cup and to introduce new tournaments, which UEFA believes would disrupt the existing international football calendar and have a negative impact on European club competitions.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Entity | Stance |
| --- | --- |
| UEFA | Against Infantino's plans |
| FIFA | In favor of expanding World Cup |
| European Nations | Threatening boycott |

> 'We cannot accept a situation where one organization takes all the decisions and the others just have to follow.' - A UEFA spokesperson.

## What's Next?
The situation is expected to escalate in the coming weeks, with UEFA and FIFA engaging in further negotiations. If an agreement cannot be reached, the boycott could have significant implications for the future of international football.

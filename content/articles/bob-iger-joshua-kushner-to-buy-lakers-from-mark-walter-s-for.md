**Executive Summary**: In a monumental deal, Bob Iger and Joshua Kushner are poised to acquire the Los Angeles Lakers from Mark Walter's ownership group for a staggering $12.5 billion, marking one of the largest transactions in sports history.

## Context / Background
The Los Angeles Lakers, one of the most iconic and successful franchises in the NBA, has been under the ownership of Mark Walter's group. However, with the emergence of new ownership, the landscape of the team and potentially the league is set to change. The acquisition by Bob Iger, known for his tenure at Disney, and Joshua Kushner, brings a new era of leadership and vision to the Lakers.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Category | Description | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase Price | The amount paid for the acquisition of the Lakers | $12.5 Billion |
| Buyers | The individuals purchasing the Lakers | Bob Iger and Joshua Kushner |
| Seller | The current ownership group of the Lakers | Mark Walter's group |

> The sale of the Lakers to Bob Iger and Joshua Kushner represents a significant shift in the ownership landscape of the NBA, potentially influencing the team's strategy, operations, and overall competitiveness.

## What's Next?
The acquisition is expected to bring about changes in the team's management and possibly its roster. As the deal finalizes, fans and observers will be keenly watching for any moves that could impact the team's performance in the upcoming season.

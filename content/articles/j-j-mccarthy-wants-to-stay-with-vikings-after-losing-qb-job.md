**J.J. McCarthy has expressed his desire to stay with the Vikings despite losing the quarterback job to Kyler Murray.** 
## Context / Background
The Vikings recently announced Kyler Murray as their starting quarterback, leaving J.J. McCarthy as a backup. Despite this setback, McCarthy has stated that he wants to remain with the team and contribute in any way possible.

## Tactical Key Points
| Player | Position | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J.J. McCarthy | Quarterback | Backup |
| Kyler Murray | Quarterback | Starter |

> The Vikings' decision to start Kyler Murray over J.J. McCarthy has sparked debate among fans and analysts, with some questioning the team's quarterback competition.

## What's Next?
The Vikings will continue their training camp preparations, with J.J. McCarthy likely to play a backup role. The team's first game of the season is approaching, and McCarthy will need to be ready to contribute if called upon.

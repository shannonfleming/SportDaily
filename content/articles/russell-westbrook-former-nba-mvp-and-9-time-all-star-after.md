**Russell Westbrook, a former NBA MVP and 9-time All-Star, has announced his retirement from professional basketball after 18 seasons.**

## Context / Background
Russell Westbrook had a storied career in the NBA, playing for several teams including the Oklahoma City Thunder, Houston Rockets, and LA Lakers. He is known for his explosive playing style and ability to dominate games on both ends of the court.

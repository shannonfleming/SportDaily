**FIFA President Gianni Infantino is facing a crisis of trust as Norway's Lise Klaveness calls for his resignation.** 
## Context and Background
The recent developments in the world of football have led to a significant loss of trust in FIFA's leadership. Norway's Klaveness has been vocal about the need for change, emphasizing that there is no trust in the current administration. 
## Tactical Key Points
The situation is complex, with various countries and organizations weighing in on the issue. Some, like Mexico and Argentina, have expressed support for Infantino, while others, such as Norway, are demanding his resignation. 
> 'There is no trust now,' Klaveness stated, highlighting the need for a change in leadership.
## What's Next?
As the situation unfolds, it remains to be seen how FIFA will respond to the calls for Infantino's resignation. The organization is likely to face continued pressure from countries and organizations demanding change.

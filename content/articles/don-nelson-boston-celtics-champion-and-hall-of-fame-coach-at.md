**Don Nelson, a legendary figure in the NBA, has passed away at the age of 86.** 
## Context / Background
Don Nelson was a renowned American basketball player and coach, best known for his successful stint with the Boston Celtics. He won five NBA championships with the Celtics and later became a Hall of Fame coach, leading several teams including the Milwaukee Bucks, Dallas Mavericks, and Golden State Warriors.

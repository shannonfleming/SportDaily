**Mohamed Salah's potential move from Liverpool to Trabzonspor is gaining momentum.** 
## Context / Background
Mohamed Salah, the Egyptian forward, has been linked with a move away from Liverpool, and Turkish club Trabzonspor has emerged as a potential destination. According to recent reports, Trabzonspor has opened negotiations with Salah's representatives to sign the player.

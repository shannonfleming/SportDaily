**The Detroit Lions are confident that Bijan Robinson's record-breaking extension with the Atlanta Falcons will not affect their contract negotiations with running back Jahmyr Gibbs.**

## Context / Background
The NFL has recently seen a surge in high-profile contract extensions, with Bijan Robinson's 3-year, $75M max deal with the Falcons being the latest example. This development has sparked speculation about how it might impact other ongoing contract talks, including those between the Lions and Jahmyr Gibbs.

**Tony Romo, the former Dallas Cowboys quarterback and current CBS Sports analyst, has been placed on leave by the network following his arrest for operating a vehicle while intoxicated (OWI). Despite this, Cowboys owner Jerry Jones has expressed his support for Romo, believing he will 'work through' the issue.**
## Context / Background
Tony Romo has been a prominent figure in the NFL, first as a player and then as an analyst. His recent arrest has raised concerns about his future with CBS Sports. Jerry Jones, known for his loyalty to his players and staff, has come out in support of Romo, indicating that he believes Romo will overcome this challenge.

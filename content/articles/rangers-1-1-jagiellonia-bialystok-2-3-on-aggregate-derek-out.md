**Rangers' European dreams were shattered as they failed to overcome a 2-1 deficit, ultimately losing 3-2 on aggregate to Jagiellonia Bialystok in the Europa League qualifying rounds.**

## Context / Background
Rangers, under the management of Derek McInnes, were looking to bounce back from their first-leg defeat. However, their performance was not enough to secure a spot in the next round of the Europa League qualifying.

> The fans expressed their disappointment as Rangers were booed off the pitch, reflecting the team's underwhelming performance in the Europa League qualifying rounds.

## What's Next?
Rangers will now focus on their domestic campaign, looking to improve their performance and make a strong comeback in the upcoming matches. The team's winless start under Derek McInnes continues, and they will be eager to turn their fortunes around.

**Uefa has announced its intention to boycott Fifa competitions if the proposed World Cup sell-off plans proceed.** 
## Context / Background
The tension between Uefa and Fifa has been escalating due to Fifa's plans to sell off the World Cup. Uefa has expressed strong opposition to this plan, citing concerns over the potential commercialization of the sport. 
## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Entity | Stance |
| --- | --- |
| Uefa | Opposed to World Cup sell-off |
| Fifa | In favor of World Cup sell-off |
## Key Quote
> Uefa will not participate in Fifa competitions if the World Cup sell-off plans go through.
## What's Next?
Uefa is set to hold an emergency meeting to discuss the ongoing situation with Fifa. The meeting will determine the next course of action for Uefa and its member associations.

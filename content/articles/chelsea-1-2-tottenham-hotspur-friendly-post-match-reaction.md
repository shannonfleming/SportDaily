**Chelsea suffered a 1-2 defeat at the hands of Tottenham Hotspur in a friendly match.**
## Context / Background
The match was part of the Sydney Super Cup, a pre-season tournament featuring top clubs from around the world. Chelsea and Tottenham Hotspur, two of England's top Premier League teams, faced off in a highly anticipated match.
## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table

A late goal from Richarlison sealed the win for Tottenham Hotspur, giving them the Sydney Super Cup title.
> The match was a great test for both teams as they prepare for the upcoming Premier League season.
## What's Next?
Both Chelsea and Tottenham Hotspur will continue their pre-season preparations with more friendly matches before the start of the Premier League season.

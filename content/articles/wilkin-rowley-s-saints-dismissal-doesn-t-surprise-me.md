**Jon Wilkin has shared his thoughts on the recent departure of St Helens head coach Paul Rowley, stating that the decision did not come as a surprise to him.**
## Context / Background
The dismissal of Paul Rowley after just nine months at the helm of St Helens has sent shockwaves through the rugby league community. However, according to Sky Sports Rugby League's Jon Wilkin, the writing was on the wall.

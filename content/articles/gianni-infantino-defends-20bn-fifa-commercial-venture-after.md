    **Gianni Infantino, the president of FIFA, has defended the organization's $20bn commercial venture amidst mounting backlash from various stakeholders.**
    ## Context and Background
    The proposed plan involves selling the rights to the World Cup, which has sparked controversy and opposition from UEFA and other football governing bodies.
    ## Tactical Key Points and Stats

    The plan's potential financial implications and its impact on the football world are being closely scrutinized.
    > The FIFA president has stated that the plan is necessary for the growth and development of the sport, despite opposition from various quarters.

    An emergency meeting has been called by UEFA to discuss the proposal, and the situation is being closely monitored by the football community.

**Luke Littler has made history by defending his World Matchplay Darts title with a record-breaking display, thrashing Gerwyn Price 18-9 in the final.**

## Context / Background
The World Matchplay Darts is one of the most prestigious tournaments in the darts calendar, and Luke Littler's victory marks a significant milestone in his career. The tournament, held in Blackpool, saw Littler power past several opponents to reach the final, where he faced off against Gerwyn Price.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Player | Sets Won | Average |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Luke Littler | 18 | 109.28 |
| Gerwyn Price | 9 | 97.45 |

Littler's record-breaking average of 109.28 is the highest ever recorded in the World Matchplay Darts final, surpassing Phil Taylor's previous record.

## Key Quote
> 'I'm over the moon to have defended my title and broken the record for the highest average in the final. It's an incredible feeling and I'm so grateful to have had the support of the crowd and my team.' - Luke Littler

## What's Next?
Littler's victory marks a significant moment in his career, and he will be looking to build on this success in upcoming tournaments. The darts calendar is packed with exciting events, and fans will be eager to see if Littler can continue his winning streak.

**J.J. McCarthy is undeterred in his pursuit of becoming an NFL starter.**
## Context / Background
The NFL has seen numerous quarterback changes in recent times, with J.J. McCarthy being one of the notable names. Despite losing the QB job to Kyler Murray, McCarthy remains optimistic about his chances of starting in the league.
## Tactical Key Points
McCarthy's determination and skillset are key factors that could influence his future in the NFL. With the Vikings' recent decision, it will be interesting to see how McCarthy adapts and potentially finds a new team to showcase his abilities.
> McCarthy's confidence in his abilities is a crucial aspect of his potential success in the NFL.
## What's Next?
As the NFL season progresses, all eyes will be on McCarthy to see how he navigates this challenging situation. With several teams potentially looking for a quarterback, McCarthy's chances of finding a new home and proving himself as a starter are still very much alive.

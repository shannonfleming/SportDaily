**Carson Beck's impressive start to the NFL preseason has drawn praise from coaches and fans alike.** 
## Context / Background
The NFL preseason is a critical period for teams to assess their players and make key decisions about their rosters. For the Arizona Cardinals, one player who has been making waves is quarterback Carson Beck. 
## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
Beck's performance in the Hall of Fame Game against the Panthers was particularly noteworthy, with many calling for him to be considered as a starter for the upcoming season.
> The same thing I keep seeing in practice, that's what I saw in the game, said Mike LaFleur, highlighting Beck's consistency.
## What's Next?
As the preseason continues, all eyes will be on Beck to see if he can maintain his current level of performance. The Cardinals' coaching staff will be closely evaluating his progress and deciding whether he is ready to take on a more significant role in the team.

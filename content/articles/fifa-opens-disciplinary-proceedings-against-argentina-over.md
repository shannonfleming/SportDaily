**Fifa has initiated disciplinary proceedings against Argentina due to a Falklands banner and chaos during the final.** 
## Context / Background
The 2026 World Cup has been marred by controversy, with the latest incident involving Argentina and a banner claiming the Falklands are Argentine. This move has prompted Fifa to open disciplinary proceedings against the Argentine Football Association. The Falklands have been a point of contention between Argentina and the United Kingdom for decades.

## What's Next?
The disciplinary proceedings will likely involve an investigation into the incidents and potential penalties for the Argentine Football Association. The outcome of these proceedings could have significant implications for Argentina's participation in future international competitions.

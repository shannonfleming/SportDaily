**Los Angeles Dodgers executives, Andrew Friedman and Dave Roberts, have come out in defense of their team amid criticisms that they are ruining baseball.**

## Context / Background
The Dodgers have been at the center of controversy in recent times, with many critics accusing them of disrupting the competitive balance in Major League Baseball. The team's significant financial resources and willingness to spend on top talent have led to accusations that they are buying their way to success.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
While the Dodgers have indeed been aggressive in the free agent market, their executives argue that this is simply a result of their commitment to winning. They point to the team's strong farm system and player development program as evidence that they are dedicated to building a sustainable winner.

> We're not trying to ruin baseball, we're just trying to win, said Friedman in a recent interview.

## What's Next?
The Dodgers will continue to be under scrutiny as the season progresses. With several key players set to become free agents in the coming years, the team will need to make some tough decisions about how to allocate their resources. Fans will be watching closely to see how the team navigates these challenges.

**The US Senate has postponed the vote on the Protect College Sports Act until September, leaving the future of college sports uncertain.**

## Context / Background
The Protect College Sports Act aims to provide a framework for college athletics, addressing issues such as player compensation and conference realignments. The bill has been a subject of debate, with various stakeholders weighing in on its potential impact.

| Potential Impact | Affects college athlete compensation and conference realignments |

> The delay in the vote has raised questions about the future of college sports, with some experts speculating that it may lead to further conference realignments and changes in the college athletics landscape.

## What's Next?
The Senate's decision to delay the vote until September means that college sports will continue to operate under the current framework for the time being. The upcoming vote will be closely watched, as it has the potential to significantly impact the future of college athletics.

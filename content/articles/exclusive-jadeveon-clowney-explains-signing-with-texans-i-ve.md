**Jadeveon Clowney has signed with the Houston Texans again, marking a significant reunion for the team and the player.** 
## Context / Background
Jadeveon Clowney, the talented pass rusher, has decided to reunite with the Texans, the team that initially drafted him in 2014. This move comes after Clowney's stint with other teams, and it seems he has found his way back home. The decision to sign with the Texans again was reportedly 'a really easy decision' for Clowney.

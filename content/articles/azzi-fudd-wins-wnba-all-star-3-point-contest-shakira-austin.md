**Azzi Fudd made history by becoming the first rookie to win the WNBA 3-point shootout at WNBA All-Star weekend.**
## Context / Background
The WNBA All-Star weekend featured the highly anticipated 3-point contest, with several top players competing for the top spot. Azzi Fudd, a rookie, surprised many by taking home the title.
## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Player | Team | 3-Point Percentage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Azzi Fudd | Dallas Wings | 44.1% |
| Shakira Austin | Washington Mystics | 42.9% |
> The 3-point contest was filled with excitement, with Azzi Fudd edging out her competitors to take the win.
## What's Next?
The WNBA All-Star weekend will continue with more exciting events, including the Shooting Stars Challenge, which was won by Shakira Austin, Elena Delle Donne, and GG Banks.

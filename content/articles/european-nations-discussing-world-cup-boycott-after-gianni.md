**European nations are considering a drastic move to boycott the World Cup in response to FIFA's controversial plan to sell shares in a spin-off venture.**

## Context / Background
The recent announcement by FIFA president Gianni Infantino to sell shares in a spin-off venture has sparked outrage among European nations. The plan, which aims to attract external investors, has been met with fierce opposition from UEFA and other football governing bodies.

## What's Next?
As the situation continues to unfold, European nations will be closely monitoring FIFA's next move. The potential boycott of the World Cup could have significant implications for the tournament and the sport as a whole.

**Top FIFA officials Wenger and Grafström have distanced themselves from Infantino's World Cup sell-off plan, signaling a significant divide within the organization.**
## Context / Background
The recent proposal by Gianni Infantino to sell off the World Cup has sparked controversy and opposition from various quarters. The plan, which aims to commercialize the event further, has been met with skepticism by many, including top officials within FIFA.
## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
| Official | Stance on Sell-Off Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Wenger | Opposed |
| Grafström | Opposed |
| Infantino | In Favor |
> The opposition from top officials like Wenger and Grafström indicates a deep-seated concern about the direction FIFA is heading under Infantino's leadership.
## What's Next?
As pressure grows, Infantino has called an emergency FIFA staff meeting to discuss the future of the organization and the proposed sell-off plan. The meeting is expected to address the concerns of officials and members, and a decision on the plan's fate may be announced soon.

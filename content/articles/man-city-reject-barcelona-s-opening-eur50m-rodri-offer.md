**Manchester City has rejected Barcelona's opening offer of €50m for midfielder Rodri, with the club considering the bid too low.**

## Context / Background
The Spanish midfielder has been a key player for Manchester City since joining the club in 2019. With interests from top European clubs like Barcelona and Real Madrid, Rodri's future has been subject to speculation. Manchester City is keen to retain the services of their star midfielder and is not willing to sanction a cut-price deal.

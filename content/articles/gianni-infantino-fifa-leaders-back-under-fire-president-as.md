**FIFA President Gianni Infantino has received backing from the organization's leaders following a crisis meeting, despite facing criticism over the World Cup sell-off.**

## Context / Background
The crisis meeting was held to address the controversy surrounding the World Cup sell-off, which has put Infantino's presidency under scrutiny. The meeting saw Infantino apologize for 'mistakes' made during the process, in a bid to salvage his position as the head of FIFA.

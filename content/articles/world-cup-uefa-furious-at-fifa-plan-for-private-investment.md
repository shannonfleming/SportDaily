    **Uefa and Fifa are at odds over a proposed plan to allow private investment in the World Cup, with Uefa expressing strong opposition to the idea.**
    ## Context / Background
    Fifa's plan, led by Gianni Infantino, aims to sell stakes in the World Cup to private investors, potentially generating significant revenue for the organization. However, Uefa has come out strongly against the proposal, citing concerns about the potential impact on the sport.
    ## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
    | Organization | Position on Private Investment |
    | --- | --- |
    | Uefa | Opposed |
    | Fifa | In Favor |
    ## Key Quote
    > 'Fifa's plan to sell stakes in the World Cup is a nuclear bomb for football,' said a Uefa spokesperson.
    ## What's Next?
    The proposal is set to be discussed further in upcoming meetings between Fifa and Uefa, with the potential for significant changes to the World Cup's commercial structure.

**Jonathan Taylor has agreed to a two-year extension with the Indianapolis Colts, worth $44 million.** 
## Context / Background
The running back has been a key player for the Colts, and this extension solidifies his position with the team. The deal comes after Bijan Robinson's historic contract with the Falcons, which may have influenced the negotiations.

The Colts will be looking to utilize Taylor's skills to improve their performance in the upcoming season. With this extension, the team has secured a key player and can now focus on other areas of their roster.

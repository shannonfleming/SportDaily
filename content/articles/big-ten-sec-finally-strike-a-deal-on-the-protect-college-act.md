**The Big Ten and the Southeastern Conference (SEC) have reached an agreement on the Protect College Sports Act, marking a significant step forward in the legislative process.**

## Context / Background
The Protect College Sports Act aims to provide a framework for the governance of college sports, addressing issues such as player compensation, revenue distribution, and athletic department oversight. The bill has been the subject of intense debate and negotiation, with various stakeholders, including the Big Ten and the SEC, playing a crucial role in shaping its provisions.

## Tactical Key Points / Stats Table
The agreement between the Big Ten and the SEC is expected to pave the way for the passage of the bill in the Senate. Key points of the agreement include:
* Support for the bill's core provisions, including the establishment of a national governing body for college sports
* Commitment to work together to address outstanding issues and concerns
* Recognition of the need for flexibility and adaptability in the governance of college sports

> The agreement between the Big Ten and the SEC is a significant step forward for the Protect College Sports Act, and we are pleased to see these two major conferences working together to support the bill.

## What's Next?
The passage of the Protect College Sports Act is expected to have far-reaching implications for college sports, and its impact will be closely watched by stakeholders across the country. The bill's proponents argue that it will help to ensure the integrity and sustainability of college sports, while its critics argue that it will impose undue burdens and restrictions on athletic departments and conferences.

    ## Context / Background
    Cristian Romero's move to Atletico Madrid has been highly anticipated, with the Argentine defender completing a £34.2m transfer. This move marks the end of Romero's tenure at Tottenham, where he served as the team captain.

    | Team | Transfer Fee |
    | --- | --- |
    | Atletico Madrid | £34.2m |
    Romero's transfer to Atletico Madrid is expected to bolster the team's defense, with the Argentine defender bringing his experience and skills to the Spanish club.
    > I wasn't perfect, but I gave it my all during my time at Tottenham.

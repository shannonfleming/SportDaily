**Jackson Koivun has made history by winning his first PGA Tour title in just his third start as a professional.**
## Context / Background
The 3M Open in Minnesota witnessed a thrilling finish as Jackson Koivun held off world No 1 Scottie Scheffler to secure a three-stroke victory. This win marks a significant milestone in Koivun's career, demonstrating his potential as a rising star in the golf world.
## Tactical Key Points
Koivun's strategy and skill on the course were key to his success, as he managed to maintain his lead despite Scheffler's charge. The tournament showcased the competitive spirit of both players, with Koivun ultimately emerging victorious.
